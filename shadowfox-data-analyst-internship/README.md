# 📊 ShadowFox Data Analyst Internship

Welcome to my **ShadowFox Data Analyst Internship** project repository.

This repository contains the projects I completed during my Data Analyst Internship across three progressive levels: **Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced**.

The projects demonstrate my practical experience in **data cleaning, data analysis, SQL, Python, Excel, Power BI, data visualization, dashboard development, and business insights**.

---

## 🎯 Internship Overview

The internship projects were completed progressively to develop and demonstrate Data Analytics skills at different levels of complexity.

The overall workflow followed throughout the projects was:

**Data → Cleaning → Analysis → Visualization → Insights → Recommendations**

Each level focuses on increasing analytical complexity, from spreadsheet-based analysis to deeper data analysis and advanced business intelligence dashboards.

# 📈 Data Analytics Approach

Across the internship projects, I followed a structured approach to solving data problems.

### 1. Understand the Data

Identify the available fields, business context, and analytical objectives.

### 2. Clean and Prepare

Handle data-quality issues and prepare the dataset for analysis.

### 3. Analyze

Use Excel, Python, and SQL to explore the data and identify important patterns.

### 4. Calculate KPIs

Develop relevant metrics to measure business performance.

### 5. Visualize

Create charts and interactive dashboards to communicate findings effectively.

### 6. Generate Insights

Translate analytical results into meaningful business observations.

### 7. Recommend Actions

Use the findings to suggest practical business improvements.
